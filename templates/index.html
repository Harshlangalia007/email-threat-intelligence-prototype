DUAL-MODULE SOC ARCHITECTURE

Module 1 — Threat & Fraud Engine: Analyzes identity spoofing, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, social engineering coercion cues, and static URL structures to output an explainable 0–100 risk score.

Module 2 — Forensic Origin & Infrastructure Engine: Reconstructs the multi-hop relay path, isolates the earliest reliable sending MTA, enriches ASN/ISP intelligence, and renders an interactive relationship graph.

ATTRIBUTION PRINCIPLE & CAVEATS

Strict SOC Attribution Standard: The platform explicitly separates observed sending infrastructure from the attacker's physical location.

Attackers routinely route campaigns through cloud providers, VPNs, Tor nodes, or compromised relays. The system explicitly declares confidence levels and warns analysts against premature physical attribution.
